Questions parents ask

Why a student tutor instead of an adult?

I took these classes recently, so I know the current curriculum, teachers' expectations and the AP exam format firsthand. Many students also find it easier to ask a near-peer questions.

What grades do you work with?

Elementary through high school. Rates are $30 per hour for elementary school (through 6th grade), $35 for middle school (7th–8th) and $40 for high school. Calculus and AP subjects are best for high schoolers.

Can you help with AP exam prep?

Yes. I earned a 5 on six AP exams and can help with content review, free-response practice and test strategy.

Where do sessions happen?

I prefer meeting in person, either at the Rossmoor Library or in the student's home. I also tutor online over Google Meet.

What if my student needs ongoing help?

Many families book a weekly session. The 4-session package is the best value for regular help.
